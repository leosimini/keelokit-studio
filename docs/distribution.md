# Distribution proposals

Studio packages only ready pieces with independent passing QA and an exact approved plan. Internal piece and variant IDs remain separate from public titles and names. Every variant points to the byte hash in the QA-bound export manifest.

Channel placements or series are proposals and require a declared verified capability for that channel concept. A distribution package always starts as `not-published`; creating it performs no remote write and does not imply that a platform accepted the media.
