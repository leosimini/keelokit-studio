# Export and independent QA

Export packages contain only requested variants. Each artifact is identified by its bytes and receives mechanical presence, format and decodability checks. Clean, captioned, audio and subtitle variants are requested explicitly; unsupported media needs a measured external probe result rather than an inferred pass.

QA is a separate record authored by an identified reviewer. It preserves mechanical checks, visual/auditory observations, reviewed evidence, blockers, verdict and inspection limits. Render or export success alone never produces a passing verdict, and incomplete media inspection is recorded as a limitation.
