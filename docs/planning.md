# Objective-led content plans

Studio accepts explicit suite and piece candidates derived from the approved-scope brief. It assigns stable internal IDs and validates audiences, evidence, dependencies, priority, order, and known budget. It does not add channels, audiences, platforms, or a fixed number of pieces.

Every planned piece records its primary audience, editorial intention, message, CTA, evidence on hand and still required, structure, duration estimate, formats, language, channels, order, dependencies, requirements, estimated cost, and review criteria. The plan also keeps pending and out-of-scope work visible.

Plan creation proposes production work only. It does not create media, imply that required evidence exists, or authorize a batch.
