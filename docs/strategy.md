# Content strategy and publication queue

Studio persists strategy separately from the production plan. Each candidate binds one planned piece and channel to cited audience/evidence inputs, versioned recipes, an impact hypothesis, uncertainty, effort basis, blockers, alternatives and explicit publication dependencies. Plan order is not accepted as strategic reasoning.

The ranking policy is deterministic: objective fit, expected impact, confidence, known effort and stable candidate identity. Scores are bounded editorial judgments, not performance forecasts. Unknown effort stays `null`; publishing, QA or critic approval never creates an observed outcome.

Publication readiness is derived from the current ready piece, validated distribution package, channel package, dependencies, explicit blockers and publication history. The queue is a pure recommendation; publication remains a separate explicit action. Already published candidates require an explicit republication rationale.

Recipes are versioned operating hypotheses backed by [the source register](../references/content-practice-sources.md). Normative accessibility requirements, research findings, platform guidance and practitioner heuristics retain distinct evidence classes and limitations.
