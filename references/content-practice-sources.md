# Content practice sources

These sources inform versioned Studio recipes. They do not guarantee engagement or conversion. Applicability, assumptions, limitations and observed outcomes remain explicit.

| ID | Source | Evidence class | Encodable guidance | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SRC-POSITIONING-DUNFORD | [April Dunford — A Quickstart Guide to Positioning](https://www.aprildunford.com/post/a-quickstart-guide-to-positioning) | practitioner framework | Relate current alternatives and differentiated capabilities to customer value and best-fit buyers. | Not peer reviewed; do not encode unsourced numeric claims. |
| SRC-USER-NEEDS-GOVUK | [GOV.UK — Identify user needs](https://guidance.publishing.service.gov.uk/writing-to-gov-uk-standards/plan-manage-content/identify-user-needs/) | authoritative operational guidance | Anchor content in an evidenced user task, situation and desired outcome; label assumptions. | Public-service governance does not transfer verbatim. |
| SRC-MULTIMEDIA-MAYER | [Cambridge Handbook of Multimedia Learning — reducing extraneous processing](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/cambridge-handbook-of-multimedia-learning/principles-for-reducing-extraneous-processing-in-multimedia-learning-coherence-signaling-redundancy-spatial-contiguity-and-temporal-contiguity-principles/C98AB3A6CE760DD63C048936EA0B3B44) | research synthesis | Remove irrelevant decoration, signal key ideas and align narration with the relevant visual in time and space. | Research is primarily instructional; marketing transfer is a hypothesis. |
| SRC-VIDEO-BRAME | [Brame — Effective Educational Videos](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5132380/) | peer-reviewed review | Segment explanations, manage cognitive load and connect video to an application or next action. | Education context; no universal marketing duration follows. |
| SRC-VIDEO-GUO | [Guo, Kim and Rubin — How video production affects student engagement](https://doi.org/10.1145/2556325.2566239) | large observational study | Prefer focused segments and measure actual retention; split at meaningful boundaries. | MOOC data from 2014; observational association, not a causal universal limit. |
| SRC-WCAG-MOTION | [WCAG 2.2 — Animation from interactions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions) and [Pause, Stop, Hide](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide) | normative accessibility standard/guidance | Support reduced motion for nonessential triggered animation and controls for qualifying automatic motion. | Apply the exact criterion scope and conformance level. |
| SRC-WCAG-CAPTIONS | [WCAG 2.2 — Captions prerecorded](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/captions-prerecorded) | normative accessibility standard | Prerecorded synchronized media requires accurate captions for speech and relevant audio, subject to the stated exception. | Automated captions still require review. |
| SRC-YOUTUBE-RETENTION | [YouTube — Measure key moments for audience retention](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415?hl=en) | first-party platform guidance | Inspect intro retention, dips, spikes and end retention for comparable uploads. | YouTube-specific and descriptive; it does not prove a hook formula. |
| SRC-EXPERIMENT-MICROSOFT | [Microsoft Research — Online experimentation](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/online-experimentation-at-microsoft/) and [pre-experiment patterns](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/articles/patterns-of-trustworthy-experimentation-pre-experiment-stage/) | research and large-scale practice | Define hypothesis, primary outcome, guardrails, assignment, data quality and decision rule before testing. | Small content samples may lack power or independence. |
| SRC-EDITORIAL-GOVUK | [GOV.UK — Send standard content for review](https://guidance.publishing.service.gov.uk/publish-update-retire-content/standard-content-types/send-standard-review/) and [manage existing content](https://guidance.publishing.service.gov.uk/writing-to-gov-uk-standards/plan-manage-content/manage-existing-govuk-content/) | authoritative operational guidance | Use second-eye review, preview before publication, record change rationale and refresh from user-performance issues. | Workflow details are organization-specific. |

Studio recipes distinguish normative requirements from research findings, platform guidance and practitioner heuristics. Unknown performance remains unknown; a published flag, QA pass or critic score is never an observed business outcome.
